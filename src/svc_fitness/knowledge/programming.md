# Programming: how much, how hard, how often

Source keys resolve in `../library/SOURCES.md`. `(coach rule of thumb)` = common practice, not tested by a cited study.

## Effort: RIR and RPE

- **RIR** = reps left in reserve at the end of a set. RPE 10 = 0 RIR, RPE 8 = 2 RIR. [helms2016]
- Adjust load set by set: if the first set ends at RIR 4 instead of 2, add load. [helms2016]
- Default for athletes in season: RIR 2-3 on main lifts. Failure is rarely needed. (coach rule of thumb)
- Training to failure did not consistently change outcomes for healthy adults in ACSM's 2026 update. [acsm2009]
- Self-rated RIR is less accurate for beginners and at high reps. Video `velocity_loss` is a check, not a replacement. (coach rule of thumb)

## Sets x reps by goal

| Goal | Sets x reps | Effort / load | Rest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Max strength | 3-5 x 3-5 | RIR 2-3 (about 80-90% 1RM) | 2-5 min [suchomel2018] |
| Power (lift fast) | 3-5 x 2-3 | 30-80% 1RM, every rep at full intent | 2-3 min |
| Pulling derivatives | 3-5 x 2-3 | can exceed the clean 1RM (pulls) [suchomel2015] | 2-3 min |
| Hypertrophy | 2-4 x 6-12 | RIR 1-3 | 1-3 min |
| Tendon isometric | 4-5 x 30-45 s | as heavy as pain allows [rio2015] | 1-2 min |
| Plyometric | 3-5 x 3-6 contacts (high), 10-20 (pogo) | quality, full recovery | 1-3 min |

- Load ranges and rest for hypertrophy and plyo rows are (coach rule of thumb).
- Weaker athletes: build strength first. Stronger athletes: shift toward power while keeping strength. [suchomel2018]
- Core principles: progressive overload, specificity, variation. [acsm2009]

## Microdosing

**What it is**

- Split the week's volume into frequent, short bouts (often 10-20 min). [cuthbert2024]
- It is not the same as the minimum effective dose. [cuthbert2024]

**What the evidence shows**

- Direct trials are few. The case is mostly inferred from scheduling, motor-learning and individualisation research. [cuthbert2024]
- Do not claim it is better than normal sessions. Say it fits a busy week and may help adherence. [cuthbert2024]

**How to use it**

- 1-2 sets of 1-3 exercises, RIR 3, done before practice or at home. (coach rule of thumb)
- Keep one heavier exposure per pattern per week when possible. (coach rule of thumb)

## Velocity loss (VBT)

- Stop a set when rep speed drops by a set percentage from the fastest rep. [weakley2021]
- Squat, 20% vs 40% loss: similar strength with ~40% fewer reps, bigger jump gains with 20%. [pareja2017]
- 40% gave more hypertrophy but shifted fibres toward slower types. [pareja2017]
- In season or for power: cap at about 20%. For hypertrophy blocks: 30-40% is acceptable. (coach rule of thumb, based on pareja2017)
- On phone video we only measure **concentric time** (`velocity_loss` = rep-time slowing across the set). That is a proxy, not a bar-speed sensor. (coach rule of thumb)

## Concurrent training (strength + conditioning + basketball)

- Concurrent training reduced strength and hypertrophy gains vs strength alone. [wilson2012]
- Running interfered more than cycling; more frequent and longer endurance work interfered more. [wilson2012]
- Practical: keep conditioning on the bike when possible; put heavy legs and hard running on the same day, then rest, rather than on alternate days. (coach rule of thumb)
- Lift before court work if the lift is the priority that day. (coach rule of thumb)

## Deloads

- A deload is a planned period of lower training stress to shed fatigue and restore readiness. [bell2023]
- There are no objective data on how often to deload. [bell2023]
- Typical practice: every 4-8 weeks, or when performance drops and soreness lingers. Cut sets about 30-50% and keep intensity. (coach rule of thumb)
- In season, a light week around a heavy game stretch counts as a deload. (coach rule of thumb)

## Tempo

- Rep durations from 0.5 to 8 s gave similar hypertrophy when sets reached failure. [schoenfeld2015tempo]
- Very slow reps (over 10 s) may be worse for growth. [schoenfeld2015tempo]
- Use slow eccentrics (3 s) for technique and tendon-friendly loading. Lift the concentric fast for strength and power. (coach rule of thumb)
- `tempo` on video = eccentric and concentric seconds per rep. Check it against the prescription.
