## 1. Two of three turns stall at the cone

![The block stops at the right cone for 1.5 s](clip:00:02-00:08)

**What the video shows**
- **00:04**: stops at the right cone for **1.5 s** before running back
- **00:14**: the same stall, **1.5 s** again

![The same stall at 00:14](clip:00:13-00:18)

**Why it happens**
- likely: it arrives at full speed and has to stop dead before it can turn
- unclear: the frames cannot show the feet

**The correct model**
- **two short steps, plant the outside foot, push back**

**Drill**
- **cone turns: 6 × 10 m**, say "short, short, plant" each rep

**How to check next time**
- every turn **under 0.8 s**: the four legs then take **under 18.5 s** (now 19.5 s)
